# Privacy Policy — Wallreel

_Last updated: 2026-06-09_

Wallreel ("the app") is developed by efesert. This privacy policy explains how the
app handles your information.

## Summary

**Wallreel does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**
The app has no internet access, no analytics, no ads, and no accounts.

## Photos you add

When you add photos or import a folder, the app copies the selected images into its
own private storage on your device so they can be used as wallpapers. These copies:

- never leave your device,
- are not uploaded to any server,
- are not shared with the developer or any third party.

When you remove a photo or delete an album, the corresponding copies are deleted
from the app's private storage.

## Permissions

- **Receive boot completed** — used only to restore your wallpaper change schedule
  after the device restarts.
- **Photo/folder access** — handled through Android's system picker. The app only
  receives the specific images or folder you choose; it has no broad access to your
  gallery.

The app does **not** request internet access.

## Data sharing

No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

## Children

The app does not collect any data and is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted in the app's GitHub
repository.
